# Gridiron HQ — the plan, where it stands, and what's still missing (2026-09-07)

Written the night before the Matta–Kodsi draft (Mon Sep 7, 7:00 PM ET, 8-team PPR, slot 2),
after a day of parallel builds. Everything below carries the number that justifies it; anything
without a number is labelled as such.

## The architecture (layers, bottom to top)

1. **Data** — ESPN (league, players, ADP/injury/projections, Rotowire notes), nflverse
   (play-by-play features, rosters, depth charts, PFR advanced, NGS, QBR, schedules with closing
   lines, draft picks, contracts), FFC/Sleeper ADP, FantasyCalc, analyst takes (88 players,
   dated/sourced). `off_player_season_features`: 66 columns, 2021–2026. `docs/OFFSEASON_DATA.md`.
2. **Models** (each walk-forward validated, each with a declined list):
   - Career record (`player-career.js`): season-by-season lines, finishes, streaks; points validated
     vs ESPN r=0.9987.
   - Preseason projection (`preseason-model.js`): **learned models do not beat the market.** Shipped
     = calibrated rank→points curve + tier bands + drivers. `docs/PRESEASON_MODEL.md`.
   - Offseason changes (`offseason-model.js`): team change ×0.82, depth demotion ×0.71, 4+ games
     missed ×0.92 shipped; "emptied room" falsified. `docs/OFFSEASON_MODEL.md`. **v2 on the 66
     features: 39 variables tested, none improved the published multiplier held-out (0/3 seasons
     on share and on PPG) → v1 stays.** The receiving-charting block (air-yard share, YAC over
     expected, broken tackles) is the one additive control (−0.0053 SIG) and belongs in the
     preseason projection, not in a change multiplier — next iteration there.
   - Draft audit 2021–25 (`draft-audit-signals-2026.json`): RB over WR at overall 13–36 (t=3.4),
     rookie-WR premium (t=3.4), realization multipliers by tier. `docs/DRAFT_AUDIT_2021_2025.md`.
   - In-house season model (`projections.js`, 2025 held-out MAE 42.9 vs 47.0) — used as a relative
     nudge (weight 0.4) on ESPN's line. **Open item:** my 1-season test showed +0.04 Spearman for the
     blend; the preseason agent's 3-season test showed it winning 1 of 3. Not settled — see below.
3. **Draft engine** (`draft-assist.js`, `draft-lookahead.js`): value over an *expected* replacement
   (survival model), pair-aware horizons for slot 2's 15/18, 31/34, tier cliffs, handcuffs,
   late-round ceiling bets, need-aware "gone by my turn" (fit on 280 real picks, LOO log-loss
   0.166/0.159 vs 0.182/0.177), K/DEF only when a slot is open, Monte Carlo lookahead (200 sims ×
   6 candidates, ~1 s).
4. **Advisor** (Claude, `drafts.js` advice route): full season table per candidate, offseason
   read, preseason drivers, ESPN/Rotowire note, analyst takes; instructed to lead with a number.
5. **Live mirror**: ESPN's REST draft view is **frozen during a live draft** (confirmed by four
   independent projects and by tonight's Ajay's-league draft: 0/178 picks mirrored). Picks flow
   only over the draft-room WebSocket. New: in-tab bookmarklet taps the page's own socket →
   `POST /api/drafts/:id/capture` → reconciler; REST poll pauses while the tab feeds.
   `docs/DRAFT_CAPTURE.md`.
6. **Access**: phone via Cloudflare tunnel + one-time pairing code; always-on launcher service
   (launchd) that can start the app from a phone.
7. **Betting engine** — *not yet fed by any of this.* `docs/OFFSEASON_MODEL.md` §6 spells out the
   team-level contract it should consume through `model-governance.js`, gated.

## Draft-night runbook (Sep 7)

1. Mac awake; `npm run tunnel` running (or tap the launcher bookmark on the phone).
2. Open the ESPN draft room on the laptop FIRST. In Gridiron HQ → Live Draft → "Get the ESPN tab
   bookmarklet" → drag it to the bookmarks bar → click it once in the ESPN tab. Pill turns green.
   **Never reload the ESPN tab** (a reload can cost the seat); if you must, click the bookmarklet
   again.
3. Dry-run first in an ESPN mock draft (any time before 7 PM) — the bookmarklet's DOM selectors and
   the `MessageEvent.data` patch are from research, not yet observed on a live room.
4. Draft in ESPN. Read Gridiron HQ. Advice prefetches 3 picks out; the lookahead card says which
   candidate leaves the best finished roster.

## Verdicts, plainly

- Live mirror: the old design could never have worked live. Fixed in principle; proven only in tests.
- Preseason "insane machine": the market is hard to beat. What we shipped is honest calibration,
  bands, and reasons — and a ranker that uses history the market doesn't (RB-over-WR 13–36,
  rookie WRs, tier realization).
- Offseason: three real effects, one popular myth killed.
- Everything above is 739/739 green and pushed.
