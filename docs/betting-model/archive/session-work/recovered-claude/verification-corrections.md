
## verify-bet-authority-calibration
- Drop the 'second likelihood for c from CLV, multiplied with the outcome likelihood' step. The CLV slope (how the close moves given s) is a different parameter from the outcome slope c. Both are measured on the same games, so multiplying the Gaussians double-counts evidence. Keep CLV as a separate, anytime-valid monitor that can scale the Kelly multiplier kappa down.
- Remove the half-normal (non-negative) truncation on c, or at least do not fit it with a Laplace approximation. Use a symmetric prior centred at 0 and let the model bet only when the posterior-mean EV on the model's side is positive. The Gridiron evidence (-2.28 CLV, 78% adverse moves) points to negative c; truncation would hide exactly that failure while the posterior mean of c stays above zero.
- Stop stacking shrinkage layers without justification: posterior mean, then Baker-McHale lambda, then quarter Kelly, then a 2% cap. Baker & McHale (2016) say lambda is for plug-in or unbiased estimates and that a Bayes rule with a correct prior needs no extra shrinkage. Pick one uncertainty-shrinkage mechanism, either the posterior or lambda. Pre-register a single kappa, and tune it only on walk-forward folds that are never used for the final evaluation.
- Make nested out-of-sample fitting explicit (a lookahead guard). The authority logistic, conformal CDF, Venn-Abers and beta calibrator must each be fit only on base-model predictions that were themselves made walk-forward, never on in-sample fits (Benter's own requirement). With about 270 games a season, do not chain conformal CDF, Venn-Abers, beta calibration and the authority logistic together. Use one calibration layer, for example the authority logistic on s with the conformal margin CDF as the only probability source.
- Do not fit c on closing-line signals and then apply it to Tuesday or opening quotes. The historical s is computed against the close; at serving time s is computed against an earlier quote, which is a different distribution (the memory note says the opener path is dead). Either fit on quotes from the same timestamp (quote tape only, so authority stays near zero until that sample grows) or restrict model bets to near-close quotes. This belongs in the recommendation, not buried in open questions.
- Move Lisandro79/BeatTheBookie to do-not-copy (GPL-3.0).
- Soften or remove the Walsh & Joshi Kelly claim and the Uhrin 'drawdown about the same' wording. Mention that distributionally robust Kelly was the safest strategy.
- Before claiming the rule turns itself off with no gate, re-run the simulation with the actual recommended specification: a and b market coefficients, the chosen prior on c, pushes, correlated slates, and a true c that is negative, to match Gridiron's CLV. Test prior misspecification too.
