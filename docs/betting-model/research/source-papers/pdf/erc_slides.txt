  • Portfolio resampling (Michaud, 1989)
  • Robust asset allocation (Tütüncü and Koenig, 2004)

⇒ Market practice: many investors prefer more heuristic solutions,
which are computationally simple to implement and appear robust as
they are not dependent on expected returns.

  • The minimum variance (mv) portfolio
    It is obtained for φ = 0 in the mean-variance problem and does
    not depend on the expected returns.
  • The equally-weighted (ew or 1/n) portfolio
    Another simple way is to attribute the same weight to all the
    assets of the portfolio (Bernartzi and Thaler, 2001).

⇒ EMN HF exhibits a beta against equity indices closed to zero.

In general, EMN is a pure stock picking process: the strategy holds
Long / short equity positions, with long positions hedged with short
positions in the same and in related sectors, so that the equity
market neutral investor should be little affected by sector-wide events.

Traditional asset allocation ⇒ concentrated portfolios
