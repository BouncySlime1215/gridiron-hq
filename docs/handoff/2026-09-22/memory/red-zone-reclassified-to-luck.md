moves from Phase A item 2 (predictive feature) to item 14 (luck
decomposition).

Two independent methods agree: it describes past outcomes, not future ones.

- Model-evidence audit ([[gridiron-phase-a-feature-verdicts]]): redundant
  with targets + snap share — absorbed, no independent lift.
- R&D: does NOT persist year-over-year for the same player. Same-season
  correlation with TD rate r=0.55, but year-over-year repeat only r=0.136.

Conclusion: descriptive of what happened, not predictive of what's next.

confidence.
