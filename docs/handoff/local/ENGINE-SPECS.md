## ACQ-01 + FLIP-01: "go get player X" planner and the flip-arbitrage radar (Nick 9/23 ~8:10 PM: "the trade rank should be WAYYY more sophisticated... a real plan towards flipping players... use the psychology... arbitrage this market... we want one player, use what we have to plan how to get there, thousands of simulations... we can't beat ESPN but we can beat my boys")
Amends CHESS-01a/b and RADAR-01a/b (same producers, sharper objective); scored on the league's goal (title default, per-league playoffs option).
**FLIP-01 (radar = the arbitrage map):** for every player, every league-mate's CLONE price (their valuation from their moves, needs, biases, activity) vs real value to each roster (title-odds contribution on the one world). Spread = max clone price - min clone price. A flip exists when A prices a player low, B prices him high, and both deals look fair on each one's own screen (ESPN/FantasyCalc). Ranked by spread x P(A accepts) x P(B accepts) x (days before the deadline). Scanned nightly and on news.
**ACQ-01 (acquisition planner):** input = a target player (or "best reachable upgrade"). Output = a path: trade / claim / flip steps across league-mates ending with the target, each step with P(accept) from the clones and its title-odds effect; searched with MCTS against clone replies on the fast rescore world (RL-19-2, thousands of paths); objective = P(end holding target) x title-odds gain - cost of assets spent; branches for "if he says no"; timing rules (sell after a spike, buy before role news, before the deadline).
**Psychology levers, labelled by evidence (only PROVEN ones steer by default; TESTING ones run in shadow until they pass):**
- PROVEN: activity -> who answers; need -> which position they trade for (not a price premium); fill-in after injury overvalued on screen (~1.2-1.5 pts/g fades); lineup gain vs paper value gap (their screen shows paper value); checked-out managers skipped.
- SIGNAL (default-off until 2026 confirms): lineup conviction (started a player over a much better one -> overprices him, RL-17-4).
- TESTING (R&D queue): primetime premium, home-team bias, offer fatigue, recency after one big game, Myerson pricing at their indifference point, offers that teach us their price.
**Offer design:** priced at the clone's indifference point, fair-looking on HIS screen, framed on his needs (Coach writes the pitch; verify.js keeps every stated fact true: the edge is his biases, never false information).
**Grading:** every sent offer and step logged (OFFER-01) and graded on outcome; clones update per reply.
Launch: after EA-00..EA-07 (one world + fast rescore) and CLONE-01b; FLIP-01's radar can start in shadow as soon as clone prices exist.
