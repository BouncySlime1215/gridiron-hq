## (c) Odds before vs after (simulateSeason, 2000 runs, seed 12345, all 5 leagues)
| league | rules the PR now reads (the old code got these wrong) | max abs change, playoff odds | max abs change, title odds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2-week rounds, playoff weeks 15-16 and 17-18 (old: 15, 16) | 0.013 | 0.028 |
| 2 | 2 divisions, winners seeded first; fixed bracket (old: re-seeded every round) | **0.000 (10/10 identical)** | 0.017 |
| 3 | 13-week regular season, 2-week rounds 14-15 and 16-17 (old: 15, 16) | 0.029 | 0.037 |
| 4 | fixed bracket (old: re-seeded) | **0.000 (10/10 identical)** | 0.010 |
| 5 | fixed bracket (old: re-seeded) | **0.000 (10/10 identical)** | 0.016 |

Why:
- **Playoff odds are unchanged wherever the simulated weeks are unchanged** (leagues 2, 4, 5 match exactly).
  - Old and new code read the same `playoffTeamCount` and `matchupPeriodCount`, so the playoff field is the same.
  - League 2's division rule cannot change its 6-team field. Two 5-team divisions mean a division winner ranks 6th at worst.
- **Leagues 1 and 3 shift slightly** because their playoff weeks changed (week 18 was added for league 1, week 14 for league 3). That changes how the random numbers are consumed, so this is Monte Carlo noise, not a rule effect.
  - The shifts (≤0.029) are smaller than the PR code's own seed-to-seed spread (seed 999 vs 12345: up to 0.049 playoff, 0.033 title).
- **Title odds change** through bracket rules the payloads state and the old code ignored:
  - `playoffReseed: false` in all 5 leagues;
  - `playoffMatchupPeriodLength: 2` in leagues 1 and 3 (two-week rounds, points summed over both weeks);
  - league 3's 13-week regular season;
  - league 2's division winners taking the top seeds and byes.
- The sizes are within Monte Carlo noise at 2000 runs, so the direction per team is not asserted. The mechanism is a rule the old code got wrong in every case.
