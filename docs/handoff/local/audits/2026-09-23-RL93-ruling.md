## (a) Method and numbers

`dump.mjs` was run once in each tree. It serves `/find` exactly as the route does (`findTrades(lg, {maxPerSide:2, requireMutual:true, limit:20, excludeIds:new Set()})`) and serves `/evaluate` with the route body (`evaluate(... , {lineupValue: lineupValueContext(lg, assets, teams)})` on the PR tree and no ctx on main). The hand-built deals are a 1-for-1 (my #2 RB/WR by value for theirs) and a 2-for-1 (my #2 and #4 for their #1) against the first other team. `cmp.py` walks both JSON trees leaf by leaf, ignoring only `runtime_ms`/`tactics_ms`.

Changed leaves by league. The change pattern is identical on every league: `me`/`them` `season_delta`, plus the 3 new fields, on every deal and every evaluate side. Nothing else changes.

What explains the changes:
- Old `season_delta` equals `ppg_delta x 17` on every side (asserted to within 0.06).
- New `season_delta` equals `[best lineup on current_week_ppg]` delta + `(W-1) x [best lineup on ros_ppg]` delta. This is recomputed independently in `audit200.mjs#check`, which solves both legs itself, and gives 0 mismatches over every find deal and evaluate side on all 5 leagues.
- Most of the change comes from the basis switch the PR describes ("this week once, weekly rate after"), not from the drop from 17 weeks to 15 or 16. The 7 sign flips are deals whose this-week gain (inside adj_ppg) and rest-of-season gain point in opposite directions.
- Every `lineup_value.total` that differs from `season_delta` is on an uneven deal. On those deals my independent recompute of the charged roster (after + replacement - dropped) matches `lv.total`. That difference is the roster-spot charge and nothing else.

`audit200.mjs` (single process, both engine copies) also showed `floor_delta`/`ceiling_delta` diffs. That is a harness artefact, not the PR. `WEEK_MARGINAL` is a per-module `Symbol` (`trade-engine.js:134`), so a copied module cannot read another module's weekly models and falls back to the `approx` spread. The two-worktree run shows floor/ceiling are unchanged.
