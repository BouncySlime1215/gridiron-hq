## Facts that shape these specs (read first)
1. **Trades are the wrong target for this-season tells.** In r18 internal, 1,504 auto tells with a timed, league-demeaned baseline gave 148 FIT survivors and 85 CONFIRMED in 2023-24 for adds and checkout. Placebo: 0/3,219. For trades beyond activity the result was a **KILL** (−0.012 [−0.022, −0.003]). With the weak pooled baseline, 784 survivors became 40, because the baseline is what makes the factory work (RL-18-2).
2. **Only prior-season trade tells can enter P(accept).** Last season's trade count, partners and trades won add +0.0137 AUC [+0.0022, +0.0251] and hold in both halves (RL-18-1). This is the explorer's CONFIRM and has **not been validator-checked**. On ESPN only `transactionCounter.trades` (a count) can be reached. `league-history.js:232` fetches the prior-season `mTeam` view, but `saveTeams` (:143) drops the counter. Local `league_roster_history` has `acquisitionType` null on all 62 rows from 2023-25, so partners and trades won are Sleeper-only.
3. **Coach's gate uses separate windows already.** `grading.js:201-202` splits the corpus at one date cut (`GRADE_SPLIT` 0.7, :56). What it lacks is any outcome: it has repeatability only (skill>0 and Spearman>=0.5, :263; "no labels", :13) and no FDR (0 hits for benjamini|fdr in coach/). The r18 critique ("passes noise": 232 passes, precision 0.116) compared overlapping cumulative windows. Per the validator, precision vs the 0.067 base rate is about 1.7x, and the true numbers are unknown until a rerun with separate windows (RL-18-3).
4. **Coach's 40 chat variables cannot be outcome-graded** (10 people, 37 decisions). Their `predictive` verdict is `untestable` and they stay unproven. Outcome grading happens only on ESPN and Sleeper streams.
5. **Where Coach gets people reads today:**
   - Coach has no people tool: `COACH_TOOLS` at tools.js:122 is catalog, sql, compute and football tools.
   - The trade card's Approach and Bait (ManagerRead.tsx:189-193, fed from counterparty-pricing.js:904-905) come from an ungraded LLM chat profile that covers at most 9 of 41 counterparties.
   - "How Nick comes across" is `selfRead` (counterparty-pricing.js:1165) → the `how_nick_looks` tactic (trade-tactics.js:592, :877) → trade-engine.js:2169. **It stays as it is; COACH-01 only reads it.**
6. **ESPN coverage:** 68 of the 85 confirmed tells can be computed from ESPN. Claim timing and FAAB tells can't be observed there. On ESPN, "checkout" means dead starts, because empty slots were 0 of 92 team-weeks. Dead starts were 2 of 92 in 2026 W1-W3, so they stay silent until byes.
7. **Reuse:**
   - BH: `benjamini_hochberg`, `scripts/model-lab/mass_test_harness.py:74` (STATS-METHOD).
   - Dead-start detection: `dead-starters.js:70 deadReason` / `:97 deadStarters`.
   - Chat-variable grading: `grading.js:183 gradeVariables`, `:296 applyGrades`.
   - R&D method to port: `rnd/loop/scripts/r18i_tells_{build,screen,confirm}.py` and `r18x_tells_prevseason.py`.
