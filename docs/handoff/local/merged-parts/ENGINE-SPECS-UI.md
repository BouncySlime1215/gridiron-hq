- **Rules for every row (shared acceptance, called "UI-RED" below):**

  3. **Calibration source:** every number shows its source through the existing `Provenance` (DesignSystem.tsx:57) and `Confidence` (:48). A field without a calibration source renders the amber guess label, or is not shown.
  4. **Byte-equal:** the rendered text equals the engine_state fixture value after formatting, and the reason chain renders every contribution with its delta.
  5. **Nav:** `navigation.ts:38-49` still lists 8 tabs (test). No deleted page is rebuilt, and no route is added to App.tsx:131-159.
  6. **UI-STANDARD 12:** screenshots of normal, empty, thin, error, 375 px and dark states, from the local server, attached to the PR. Cards follow the three-line rule (what / why / what to do), and a tap opens `Sheet` (:89) with the reason chain.
