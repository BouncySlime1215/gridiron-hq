# FEAS-140-WIRE: the points side panel and the wait-or-act switch, wired (2026-09-24)

Finishes FEAS-140 (PR #300). #300 shipped `feasibility.js#sidePanelFeasibility` and
`wait-or-act.js#waitOrActOn` as tested modules with no caller; this unit wires them
into `server/services/campaign/planner.js` and gives the side panel its own contract
key.

## 0. Decision: the view for a get-player league

One card per question, never nested:

- `feasibility` stays the league's own card (points leagues render it; the player
  card stays in `_run.feasibility_detail`, as before).
- `feasibility_points` is a new section in `plans-schema.js`: the points question on
  a league planned on title, playoff or get-player odds. A points league writes it as
  `unknown` ("already planned on points: see feasibility"); with the flag off it is
  `unknown` naming `GRIDIRON_POINTS_FEASIBILITY`.
- The side panel's full report is in `_run.feasibility_points_detail`.
- Its cost in the league's own odds is `objective_cost`, carrying the league metric's
  unit (`title_odds` or `playoff_odds`), not a key named after title odds. With no
  plan to price it is `unknown`, never 0.

## 1. Wiring

- `planLeague(adapter, { ..., env })`: `waitOrAct(step, players, { enabled: waitOrActOn(env) })`;
  when `objective.kind !== 'points'`, `sidePanelFeasibility` on the top 5 ranked plans
  (`SIDE_OPTIONS`), each with its end-state weekly lineup points, arrival week, give
  list and step count.
- The planner still never reads `process.env` itself. `settings.env` absent means
  both flags are off. `buildPlansFile` takes `env` (default `{}`); `main()` passes
  `process.env`, which is also what lets the preview switch reach the side panel.
- Behaviour change: the planner used to call `waitOrAct` with no options, which
  produced an untested "wait N days". Now it's "act" unless `GRIDIRON_WAIT_OR_ACT=1`.
