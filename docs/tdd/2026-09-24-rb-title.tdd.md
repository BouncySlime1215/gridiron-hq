## Pre-registration (written before the GREEN commit)

- **Metric.** On a synthetic 10-team league (13 regular weeks, 6-team fixed bracket
  over weeks 14-16, team lineups Normal(mean_i, 25), team 5 a longshot), 40 replicate
  seeds x 300 runs: the SD across replicates of the conditional estimate divided by
  the SD of the plain indicator estimate, per team (levels), and for team 5's paired
  title delta when it gains 6 points a week (paired arms share every draw).
- **Pass bar (r50's gate).** Median level SE ratio <= 0.5; paired delta SE ratio
  <= 0.45; no bias: for every team, |mean(conditional - plain)| < 3 SE of that
  difference, and the same for the paired delta.
- **What fails it.** Either ratio over its bar, or any bias z >= 3 (which would mean
  the within-round independence approximation leaks into the number).
- **Served numbers.** None move: `GRIDIRON_RB_TITLE` is off by default and preview
  mode does not turn it on. `shadow` carries `title_odds_rb` / `title_delta_rb`
  beside the served fields; `1` serves them.
