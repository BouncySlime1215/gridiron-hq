# BASIS-02: the title sim prices every player on the finder's rate

ONE-PLAN section 5, night 2 (handoff branch `docs/handoff/local/ONE-PLAN.md`):
"sim prices on ros_ppg (`season-sim.js:436 asofScale`, `:467 simPlayerMeans`);
RL-6-3 nulls pass", bar "`projection_basis` >= 0.9". Section 4 cause 3: RL-17-3 is
already on under preview and the check is still broken, so BASIS-02 edits the scale
path, not a flag.

## Cause (origin/main decf7eb, read from the code)

1. **The check measures the wrong pair.** `number-audit.js` builds
   `snap.projection_basis` from `buildProjections({ through: SEASON - 1 })` (last
   season's raw ppg) against `ros_ppg`. That is not what the sim prices once RL-17-3 /
   SIM-CALIB scale the pools, so no fix to the sim can ever move the row.
2. **Players with no last-season projection are not simulated.** `weekPool` returns
   null when `proj.get(p.id)` is missing (rookies, players new to the league). They
   score 0 in every simulated week while the finder rates them on `ros_ppg`
   (`test/rl-17-3-sim-ros-basis.test.js` "known limit").
3. **Two ROS producers at the current week.** With the as-of flag on (preview),
   `asofScale` levels pools on `projection-asof.js#projectionAsOf`, a second run of the
   ROS model, not on the finder's served `ros_ppg`. They should agree for players who
   played, but they are two producers, and the finder's number is the served one.

## Change (flag `GRIDIRON_BASIS_02`, '1' on, anything else off; NOT on under preview)

- A. When the sim starts at or after the finder's week, each player's level is the
  finder's `ros_ppg` when it has one; the as-of rate stays for replays of earlier weeks
  and for players the finder has no rate for.
- B. A player with a rate but no last-season projection gets a pool borrowed from the
  projected player at his position whose ppg is closest, scaled onto his rate, and is
  counted in `ros_template`. His draws stay keyed on his own id.
- C. The audit's row A compares the rate the served world actually centres each player
  on (per game played) with `ros_ppg`; an unsimulated rostered player counts as 0. The
  old last-season comparison is kept beside it as `last_season_rank_corr`. The audit is
  a measurement, so C is not behind the flag.
