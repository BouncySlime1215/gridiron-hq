## Pre-registration (written before any code)

**What is tilted.** The sim's once-per-season projection-error factor for Nick's
starting lineup: season-sim.js's team-mean term (AVAIL-HORIZON-3), one standard normal
Z per run per team, times `TEAM_MEAN_SD` (8), added to every week that team plays.
IS draws Nick's Z from a defensive mixture

    q(z) = eps * f(z) + (1 - eps) * sum_k pi_k N(z; mu_k, I),   f = N(0, I),   eps = 0.2

and weights each run by f(Z)/q(Z) over the full mixture (so every weight is <= 1/eps
= 5). Every other team's Z and every player-week draw are the nominal ones. mu_k and
pi_k (K = 2) come from a cross-entropy pilot (2 rounds x 1,000 runs) on run indices
disjoint from the main runs. When the team-mean term is off there is no season factor
to tilt, and IS says so (`status: 'inert'`) instead of serving anything.

**Metric (synthetic, in the cloud).** A 10-team league-4-shaped league (13 regular
weeks, 6-team fixed bracket over weeks 14-16, weekly lineup Normal(mean_i, 25),
team-mean term on at SD 8), Nick's team set so its direct title odds sit in 0.1-1%.
Budget 10,000 runs per estimate: IS = 2,000 pilot + 8,000 main; direct = 10,000 plain
runs. 10 seeds. Served estimator: the indicator title (RB-TITLE off, as on `main`);
the same bar is also reported, not gated, with RB-TITLE's conditional title on.

1. **Work-normalized SE ratio** = SE_IS x sqrt(wall_IS) / (SE_direct x sqrt(wall_direct)),
   wall times include the pilot. SE_IS is the independent-batch SE (20 contiguous
   batches of the main runs); SE_direct is the per-run SD from the 100k reference
   over sqrt(10,000). **Pass: <= 0.5 on every one of the 10 seeds.**
2. **Unbiased.** The mean of the 10 IS estimates vs a 100,000-run direct estimate on
   the same league: **pass if |difference| <= 2 x sqrt(SE_pooled^2 + SE_100k^2).**
3. **ESS_event** = (sum w_i v_i)^2 / sum (w_i v_i)^2 over the main runs (v_i the title
   value of run i). **Pass: >= 200 on every seed.**

**What fails it.** Any seed's ratio over 0.5, the bias test over 2 SE, or any seed with
ESS_event under 200. A fail keeps GRIDIRON_IS_TITLE off and is reported as a fail.

**Auto-off (live).** With the flag on, every IS estimate is checked against 10,000
unweighted runs on further disjoint run indices; if they disagree by more than
2 x sqrt(SE_IS^2 + SE_check^2), or ESS_event < 200, the served number stays the direct
one and the output says why (`status: 'auto_off'`).

**Served numbers.** None move by default. `GRIDIRON_IS_TITLE` unset / `0` = off (the
sim is byte-identical); `shadow` = computed beside Nick's served odds
(`title_odds_is`, `title_odds_is_se`), never served; `1` = Nick's `title_odds` level in
`simulateSeason` becomes the IS estimate when the auto-off check passes. Trade deltas
are never tilted by this unit. Preview mode does NOT turn it on.

## Result against the pre-registered bar: FAIL

`node scripts/rnd/is-title-bench.mjs --seeds 10 --ref 100000 --nick-mean 88`
(Nick's mean 88 puts his direct title odds at 0.405%, inside 0.1-1%).

| bar | indicator title (gated) | RB-TITLE conditional (reported, `--rb`) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. work-normalized SE ratio <= 0.5 on every seed | max 1.589, median 1.029: **FAIL** | max 0.708, median 0.578: FAIL |
| 2. mean of 10 IS vs 100k direct within 2 SE | 0.00399 vs 0.00405, z 0.21: pass | 0.00385 vs 0.00376, z 1.40: pass |
| 3. ESS_event >= 200 on every seed | min 34.4: **FAIL** | min 1,197: pass |
| empirical cross-seed SD, IS / direct at 10k runs | 0.547 (run count, not work) | 1.182 (IS adds noise) |

Why it misses: the only once-per-season factor the sim has for Nick's lineup is the
team-mean term (SD 8 a week). A title also needs three playoff wins, and those are
decided by week-to-week noise (SD 25 a week), which the tilt does not touch. Tilting Z
triples how often Nick's good seasons are drawn, but the title inside those seasons is
still rare, so the indicator estimate keeps a small ESS_event (34-60 of 8,000) and a
noisy batch SE. With RB-TITLE on, the bracket noise is already gone and what is left is
the pooled playoff-score sample every run shares, which IS cannot reduce: the empirical
spread across seeds is 1.18x plain RB-TITLE's, and the batch SE understates it (0.00009
reported vs 0.00013 observed), because conditional per-run values are not independent.

Decision: GRIDIRON_IS_TITLE stays off. RB-TITLE alone (plain runs) is the better
variance reducer at these odds: 10k-run cross-seed SD 0.00011 vs 0.00067 for the plain
indicator.
