# O1C-WIRE: validated O1-RADAR cells into projections.js share, through betting-fantasy-link.js

## Unit
Batch D item 14 (ONE-PLAN section 5 night 11, L0b row): validated O1-RADAR cells feed
`projections.js` share / team volume through `betting-fantasy-link.js`, behind their own flag,
only for cells that passed their gate.

## What is wired, and what is not
- The four cells the radar's own gate passed (PR #440, `FITTED_EFFECTS[*].passes_gate`):
  `teammate_out|RB`, `usage_rise|WRTE`, `star_return|RB`, `star_return|WRTE`. Watch flags never
  enter, not even the shadow.
- All four are player SHARE effects (RB: carries + targets per game; WR/TE: targets per game).
  No validated cell is a team-volume effect, so team pass / rush attempts are untouched. An RB
  delta is split over carries and targets by his own projected mix.
- The volume delta is scored with his own shrunk efficiency rates (`withOpportunityDelta`,
  exported from `projections.js`, the one producer of the weekly projection). Efficiency,
  availability and QBR terms do not move.
- Flag `GRIDIRON_O1C_WIRE`: unset / anything else = off (nothing computed, the served projection
  is the same object); `shadow` = the wired number is computed beside the served one;
  `1` = cells whose O1C gate below passed are applied. Preview mode never turns it on.
- Every cell ships `pending`, so with the flag at `1` today the served number is still unchanged.

## Pre-registration (written before the code)
- Metric, per cell: absolute error of the weekly PPR projection against the actual PPR points,
  on player-weeks where that cell fired, paired, bootstrap clustered by player (90% CI,
  `backtest-significance.js#pairedBootstrapDiff`, seed 20260925, 2,000 resamples).
- Pass bar (both, per cell):
  1. wired beats unwired: CI of (wired - unwired) error entirely below 0, n >= 30, on 2024
     player-weeks (the radar fitted its effects on 2021-23 and graded on 2024; 2025 is not read);
  2. wired beats ESPN on the same player-weeks: CI of (wired - ESPN) error entirely below 0,
     n >= 30, on 2026 player-weeks with an as-of ESPN projection captured before kickoff
     (`scripts/rnd/espn-projection-poller.mjs` JSONL).
- What fails it: either CI touching 0; n < 30; no ESPN rows for the cell (then the cell may only
  feed ROS / LOVE / flip, never the served weekly number); any served number moving with the
  flag off or in shadow; a watch flag entering the delta.
- Until a cell passes, `O1C_GATES[cell].status` stays `pending` and the flag's `1` mode applies
  nothing for it. Flipping a cell to `passed` is its own PR carrying the grade output.
