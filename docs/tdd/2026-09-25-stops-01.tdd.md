# STOPS-01: bye and injury stops with the priced trade-off

RED `<this branch's test commit>` · GREEN follows · `test/campaign-stops-01.test.js`.

Unit source: `docs/handoff/local/ONE-PLAN.md` on `claude/handoff-package-2026-09-22`,
block 14 ("STOPS-01: bye / injury stops with the priced trade-off; `stop_tradeoffs`
is served unknown today; this is its producer"), fact row 10 (week 11 is the
six-team bye cluster), and the block-1 bye-rescue lever.

## Scope

1. Find Nick's lineup holes: future weeks where his weekly lineup (the world's
   `weekly()`, the same dice every plan is scored on) sits at least 5 points
   under his own median week, past 2 SE, with starters on bye that week (a bye
   hole) or starters carrying an injury flag (an injury hole). An unexplained
   dip is not a stop.
2. Price each hole as a plan-added stop through `itinerary.js#stopTradeOff`, the
   pricing Nick's own stops already get: the cover is the best-scoring ranked
   plan (post rules) that lands by that week and adds back at least half the
   hole in that week on paired samples; cost = best plan's score minus the
   cover's. No cover: `unreachable`, with the reason, never a made-up cost.
3. Serve the rows in `stop_tradeoffs` under Coach's `tradeoffKey`
   (`add:cover_bye:<week>`, `add:custom:cover the week <w> injury hole`).

Injury-return timing per calendar week is not rebuilt here: the season sim
already prices a gap-state player's later weeks on the fitted return curve from
his role cell (`contingency.js#weeklyAvailability`, AVAIL-HORIZON). The holes
read whatever the world's weekly samples carry, so with AVAIL-HORIZON on the
injury holes follow the return curve; with it off, a flagged player's rate is
flat and the injury hole shows only through his active probability.

## Flag

`GRIDIRON_STOPS`: `1` serves; `shadow` computes into `_run.inputs.stops` only;
unset or anything else off (entry byte-for-byte unchanged). Preview mode never
turns it on.
