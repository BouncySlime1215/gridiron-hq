# U1b RB-SE: evidence (2026-09-25 18:58 EDT)

Pre-registration: `docs/tdd/U1B-RB-SE-PREREG.md` (committed first, unchanged). Stacked on #472.

## Change

`rb-title.js`: `RB_SE_BATCHES = 20`, `batchSe`, `batchPairedSe`, `batchInterval`, `batchOf`.
`meanInterval` (the independent-runs SE) is removed. `season-sim.js#rbTitleState` builds 20
batch estimators, each on its own runs' playoff-week pool. It carries each team's batch means as
`per_run.title_batches` (`title_rb_batches` in shadow). The served mean is still the full-pool one.
`tradeImpact` reads the batch means for `title_before_se` / `title_after_se`, `title_delta_se`,
`title_delta_clears_noise` and the shadow `title_delta_rb_se`. The confirm verdict's holds/shrank
split reads it through `pathExpectation.expected_se`. A flag-on world key carries `rbSe: 'batch20'`,
so the rescore cache never serves an old-SE result. With no team offsets (gap 0, the live setting),
win probabilities are counted in one pass with no sort. That is what keeps runtime at 1.24x.
Batches were chosen over a bootstrap: a bootstrap redoes the bracket integration once per resample.

### Bar 1, levels
The same sweep on five other disjoint run sets gives pooled z of 0.70, 1.01, 0.79, 0.22 and 0.32.
Plain MC at 1,200 runs against the same references gives -0.59 to 0.71. The paired RB vs plain gap
at 20k runs has pooled z 1.34. Read together: the pre-registered seeds are a tail draw, on top of a
small upward lean of the level estimate, about 0.1-0.2 SE per league (1-3% relative). The lean is
not explained yet.

### Bar 3, the two misses
- Deal 8 (RB 0 +/- 0.0001 vs 0.0003 +/- 0.0001) is rounding. `tradeImpact` rounds deltas and SEs to
  4 decimals, which is 0.01 percentage points: too coarse at 0.1-0.2% title odds.
- Deal 5 is not rounding. RB gives 0.03% vs plain 0.14% in the served world. Across 10 other worlds
  the paired RB vs plain gap is also negative (z -2.10). The likely cause is rb-title.js's one
  documented approximation: games inside one playoff round are integrated as independent. In the
  real sim they share NFL games (same-game copula, game shocks), so a trade that changes that
  correlation is mispriced.
- Also measured: across 10 worlds (seeds), RB spreads 1.4-2.4x its within-world batch SE. This is
  the fixed per-player outcome pool of each world (season-sim.js#weekPool). Plain MC shares it, and
  `odds_interval` already says it is excluded. It is not an RB error.
