# VETO-RISK: P(veto) from league 4's review history, folded into P(complete) in shadow

RED first · GREEN follows · `test/veto-risk.test.js`.

## Pre-registration (written before any code)

Unit: batch D item 24. ONE-PLAN L7 defines P(complete) = p_yes x p_survives_review,
but the planner serves p_complete = product of p_yes only (`campaign/paths.js
pathExpectation`): the implicit P(veto) today is 0. League 4 needs 5 of 8 other
owners (`vetoVotesRequired` 5, 24 h review) and has killed deals this season, so a
"yes" is not a landed trade.

`server/services/veto-risk.js` is the one producer of P(veto). It reads the
league's accepted offers from `league_transactions_raw` (the proposal, the
partner's TRADE_ACCEPT EXECUTE, the review votes, the league's TRADE_ACCEPT
PROCESS row: EXECUTED = landed, CANCELED with a veto vote = vetoed) and fits:

- arm `none`: P(veto) = 0 (what is served today);
- arm `base`: the league's veto share, Beta(1,1)-smoothed;
- arm `skew`: two buckets by the deal's absolute market-value skew (cut 15%),
  each shrunk to `base` with k = 4 pseudo-counts.

A threshold no quorum can reach gives P(veto) = 0 with basis `unreachable`.

It ships as SHADOW behind `GRIDIRON_VETO_RISK` (`1` / `shadow`; `on` is refused
down to shadow until graded; the preview switch never turns it on). With the flag
on, the producer writes `_run.inputs.veto_risk`: per served plan, each step's
P(veto) and `p_complete_with_veto` = product of p x (1 - P(veto)) beside the
served `p_complete`. No served field changes.

Metric and pass bars, fixture level (this PR):

1. Labels: landed, vetoed, in review, declined and non-veto cancels are told
   apart exactly as the fixture writes them.
2. Forward-only grade: each deal is scored only on deals decided before it
   (#319's lesson: leave-one-out flattered the baseline).
3. Shadow: the plans entry with the flag on is deep-equal to the flag-off entry
   outside `_run.inputs.veto_risk`, even with a table that vetoes everything.
4. Flag off: no `veto_risk` key anywhere in the entry.

Metric and pass bar, league level (Needs local measurement, not claimed here):
`node scripts/rnd/veto-grade.mjs --league 4` on the real DB, all seasons in the
table, forward-only, a deal scored once 5 earlier deals are decided.

- `passing` needs n_scored >= 10 AND arm `base` Brier < arm `none` Brier AND
  |mean predicted - observed veto rate| <= 0.10 for the picked arm. The pick is
  `skew` only if its Brier is at least 0.005 below `base`; otherwise `base`.
- What would fail it: `base` does not beat p = 0 (the league rarely vetoes), or
  the calibration gap exceeds 0.10. Fewer than 10 scored deals is
  `not_enough_data`, never a pass.
- Even on `passing`, switching the served P(complete) is a separate PR with
  Nick's OK: it moves every plan's expected value and the Fuck-it floor
  (`modes.js` ALL_IN_MIN_COMPLETE 0.03).
